What the sample report contains

The CreditReports.dk sample report demonstrates the structure used for Danish company checks. It is designed for B2B credit risk assessment, not personal consumer credit reports.

A standard Danish company credit report includes company identification, CVR details, financial statement history, key ratios, credit score, credit rating, bankruptcy risk, suggested credit limit, and industry comparison. The AI Credit Report adds written interpretation and a decision-support summary on top of the same core data.

CVR and Danish Central Business Register company details
Financial statements, profitability, solvency, liquidity, and efficiency ratios
Credit score, credit rating, bankruptcy risk, and credit limit indicators
AI-assisted interpretation for users who need a written credit memo
These sample reports are decision-support material for B2B credit risk assessment. They do not make or automate final credit decisions. Users remain responsible for credit policy, due diligence, legal and compliance review, and final decisions.
